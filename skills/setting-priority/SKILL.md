# Setting priority

Priority says how much a task matters next to the user's other work, not how urgent it sounded
when it arrived.

- 1: it cannot wait. Something breaks, or someone is blocked, if it is not done soon.
- 2: important this week: a promise to another person, or work that unblocks other work.
- 3: worth doing, with nothing pressing on it.
- 4: no priority. Use it when you cannot tell: it is better than a guess.
- A due date is a deadline someone set, never a wish. Without one, leave it empty.
- The star is for what the user chose to focus on today. Leave it to them.
